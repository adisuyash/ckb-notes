## Week 04

**Date:** 25th May - 1st June, 2026

### Tasks Completed / Learnings

- Continued exploring projects being built by fellow CKBuilders to better understand how different applications are leveraging the Nervos ecosystem.
- Studied the codebase and live demo of **CKB Kickstarter** (https://decentralized-kickstarter-kappa.vercel.app/) to learn how crowdfunding campaigns, project creation and user interactions are structured within a CKB-based application.
- Explored the repository structure and frontend implementation of CKB Kickstarter to gather ideas that could help improve DripFund's architecture and development approach.
- Reviewed **InheritVault** (https://inherit-vault.vercel.app/) and its codebase to understand how funds can be managed, secured and released under predefined conditions in a blockchain application.
- Compared the design and implementation choices made across both projects and noted several ideas and patterns that may be useful for future DripFund development.
- Spent time reviewing other ongoing CKBuilder projects and community feedback discussions to gain a broader understanding of common challenges, UX considerations and development practices within the ecosystem.
- Documented key learnings, observations and potential improvements that could be explored in future iterations of DripFund.
