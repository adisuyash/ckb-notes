## Week 03

**Date:** 16th May - 22nd May, 2026

### Tasks Completed / Learnings

- Explored the [Fiber Network](https://www.fiber.world/docs) architecture and studied how Layer 2 payment channels function on Nervos CKB.
- Learnt core Fiber Network concepts including **Payment Channels**, **Multi-Hop Routing** and the **Interactive Network Simulation**.
- Understood the lifecycle of payment channels including channel opening, off-chain balance updates and on-chain settlement.
- Studied how Fiber Network enables scalable and low-latency transactions by reducing Layer 1 interactions through off-chain state updates.
- Explored how multi-hop routing allows payments to travel across interconnected nodes without requiring a direct payment channel between sender and receiver.
- Tried and experimented with all the **Fiber Network simulators** to better understand channel creation, settlement, transaction routing and Layer 1 vs Layer 2 activity flow.
- Explored [Pico’s journey and the conceptual explanation of Fiber Network](https://fiber-storybook-seven.vercel.app/) through the [Fiber Storybook](https://talk.nervos.org/t/fiber-storybook-explaining-fiber-through-pico-s-airport-journey/10251) to better understand payment channel networks in a simplified and practical manner.
